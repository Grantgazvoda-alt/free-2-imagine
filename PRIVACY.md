# Privacy Policy — Orgasmo

**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you sign in with Higgsfield, we receive your user ID, workspace ID, and display name. We do not receive or store your Higgsfield password.

### Usage Data
We automatically collect:
- Pages visited and features used
- Generation prompts and outputs (stored for your history)
- Timestamps and session duration
- Browser type and device information

### Payment Data
Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers or billing details on our servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve the Service
- To display your generation history and projects
- To track usage for analytics (anonymized aggregates)
- To communicate service updates and policy changes
- To enforce our Terms of Service (e.g., prohibited content detection)

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

### Where Data Is Stored
Your data is stored on Cloudflare's global network (D1 database) and on Higgsfield's infrastructure.

### Retention Period
- Generation history: retained for the life of your account
- Analytics data: retained for 24 months
- Account data: retained until account deletion

### Deletion
You can request account and data deletion by emailing support@orgasmo.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:
- **Higgsfield:** Authentication and generation infrastructure
- **Cloudflare:** Hosting and database services
- **Stripe:** Payment processing (no card data stored by us)
- **Law enforcement:** If required by law or to enforce our Terms (Section 5 — Prohibited Content)

## 5. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:
- **Right to access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Right to rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure:** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to restrict processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to data portability:** Receive your data in a structured format
- **Right to object:** Object to processing for analytics

To exercise these rights, contact support@orgasmo.app.

**Legal basis for processing:** We process your data based on your consent (when you use the Service) and our legitimate interest in providing and improving the Service.

**Data Protection Officer:** Contact dpo@orgasmo.app

## 6. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies. Analytics data is collected via server-side logging, not client-side tracking scripts.

## 7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- All data transmitted over HTTPS
- Authentication via Higgsfield's secure platform
- Database encryption at rest (Cloudflare D1)
- No storage of passwords or payment card data

## 8. Children's Privacy

Orgasmo is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover that a user is under 18, their account will be terminated and data deleted.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be notified via email or through the Service.
